Research Interests

Reflectometry and impedance spectroscopy for fault location and condition assessment of submarine, HTS and power cables.

Signal Processing for Diagnostics

Time-frequency analysis, instantaneous frequency estimation and signal decomposition for weak and noisy measurement signals.

Prognostics and Health Management (PHM)

AI-based anomaly detection and remaining useful life prediction for power and industrial assets.

Data-Driven Energy Systems

Reinforcement learning and data-driven models for energy management, non-wires alternatives and battery lifecycle data.
